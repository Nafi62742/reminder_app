# RemindLy

> A clean, fully offline reminder & daily-routine app.  
> No cloud. No accounts. Your data never leaves your device.

---

## Features

- **Reminders** — Set time-based reminders with titles and optional notes. Get notified even when the app is closed.
- **Daily Schedule** — Build your personal daily routine checklist and reorder items with drag-and-drop.
- **Workout Tracker** — Log workout routines using preset templates or your own custom plans.
- **Completion Stats** — See your daily, weekly, and monthly check-off progress at a glance.
- **Reminder History** — Review every past reminder — completed or missed.
- **Profile** — Save your name, email, weight, and height. All stored locally on your device.
- **Backup & Restore** — Export your data to a file and restore it any time.
- **Light & Dark Theme** — Follows your system preference automatically.

## Usage

1. On first launch, enter your name to get started.
2. Use the **Reminders** tab to add time-based alerts.
3. Use the **Schedule** tab to check off your daily routine items.
4. Use the **Workout** tab to log your exercise sessions.
5. Visit the **Profile** tab to view your stats and manage your data.

---

## Privacy

RemindLy is 100% offline. No data is ever sent to any server.  
Everything you enter stays on your device.
